_A look back (and forward) as I celebrate 25 years of service with Nationwide_

### An Unlikely Parallel

It was back in the autumn of 2001 when I first walked through the doors of Nationwide House as one of the newest members of the technology trainee scheme, as it was back then. Just a couple of weeks later the world was introduced to JD, Turk, and Elliot who walked through the doors of Sacred Heart as interns.

Like those interns in Scrubs, I bumbled and stumbled my away around the real world, fresh out of education and trying to fake it til I make it. JD had Dr Cox as his long-term mentor, and I was lucky enough to start life at Nationwide under the wing of my equally quick-witted mentor, Andy.

It wasn't until this year that the parallel entered my consciousness, as Scrubs returned for a new series - this time with the former interns now in much more senior roles; the students had become the teachers.

And as I sit back and think about these parallels, it's almost uncanny.

### The Early Years

As a young buck entering the workplace at 18, going into a huge head office which housed several thousand employees was initially quite (very) daunting. But it's amazing how quickly those fears can be released when you are surrounded by good people. Working initially in desktop support, I was given the chance to get out and about and fix people's PC and laptop issues all over the building - from people in the call centre (which was then located in the head office), to HR, to  Risk, Mortgages...you name it.
